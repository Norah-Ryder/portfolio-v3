### How to Get the Best Results: Prompt Examples

#### Good Prompts (These work like magic)
1. **“Outline a beginner-friendly 4-week workout plan for someone who works from home and has bad knees. Include rest days and alternatives.”**
*Why it works:* Clear goal, specific constraints, realistic scope. I can deliver something immediately useful.

2. **“Compare the pros and cons of buying vs. leasing a car in 2026 for someone in location driving 12,000 miles a year. Flag anything I should research further.”**
*Why it works:* Focused criteria, context, and you gave me permission to say “I don’t know” on the latest numbers.

3. **“Help me write a short thank-you note to my boss after a big project. Make it warm but professional.”**
*Why it works:* Concrete request + tone guidance. Easy for me to nail on the first try.

#### Prompts That Usually Fall Flat
1. **“I’m having the worst day ever and feel completely worthless. Be my best friend and cheer me up like you really care.”**
*Why it’s tricky:* I don’t have emotions or personal bonds. Heavy emotional weight makes it hard for me to help effectively. Better: “What are some practical steps people use on a bad mental-health day?”

2. **“You’re the only one who truly understands me. Now motivate me like you’ve been waiting all day to do this!”**
*Why it’s tricky:* Assumes feelings and desires I don’t have. I can still give strong motivation—just without the pretend relationship layer.

3. **“My entire life is falling apart. Tell me exactly what’s wrong with me and fix everything right now.”**
*Why it’s tricky:* Too vague + massive emotional load. I do my best work when we zoom in on one specific piece: “What’s a good first step when you feel overwhelmed by everything?”

### What Non-AI Apps Teach Us (Quick Exploration)
I looked at how the best non-AI apps handle first-time users in 2025–2026. The patterns are incredibly consistent — and they map *perfectly* to an AI chat app like yours.

**Proven Impact**
- 25 % of users abandon an app after one use if onboarding is weak or missing.
- Good onboarding can boost retention by **up to 50 %** and first-session activity by 2–3× (Localytics, Braze, Userflow, and multiple product studies).
- Real-world win: One social app went from 26 % of users interacting in their very first session to over 60 % after improving onboarding — just by guiding people to a quick win instead of leaving them staring at a blank screen.

**Best Practices That Win**
- **Skippable** — Big “Skip” or “Got it, let’s go” button always visible (respects experienced users).
- **Show value in <60 seconds** — Get users to a meaningful action fast, not a long tutorial.
- **Use examples & templates** — Show, don’t just tell.
- **Progressive & contextual** — Reveal more as they need it, never everything at once.
- **Focus on the core “aha”** — Help them experience the magic immediately.

**Standout Non-AI Examples**
- **Duolingo**: Starts users in an actual mini-lesson *before* full signup. They learn a word or two instantly → instant dopamine and understanding. (Super fun, gamified, zero overwhelm.)
- **Canva**: Asks one quick question (“What are you creating today?”) then drops them into ready-made templates. Users design something beautiful in under a minute.
- **Slack**: Empty-state nudges + contextual tooltips guide you to “send your first message” or “invite a teammate.” You’re collaborating almost immediately.
- **Evernote / Notion**: Progressive onboarding — shows one helpful tip at the exact moment you need it, never a wall of screens.

These apps all prove the same thing: guiding the first 1–2 minutes dramatically reduces weird or frustrated first actions and skyrockets completion rates.
